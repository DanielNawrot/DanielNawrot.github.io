Computer vision control for robotics

Python, MediaPipe, PyBullet, Matplotlib

A framework that turns the motion of a user’s hand into control of a robotic arm. I split it into small projects so I could learn each fundamental by building it, then joined them into one pipeline.

  1. 2D inverse kinematics. Takes a start pose and a target point, solves for the joint angles, and animates the arm to the target.
  2. PID controller. Built from the proportional, integral and derivative terms, then tuned to compare responses to a setpoint change.
  3. Hand tracking. MediaPipe pose landmarks track the hand in real time. A button zeros the origin, and the hand’s vector from it drives the arm’s endpoint. A motion-capture mode records the path with its timing.
  4. Filtering and splines. An exponential moving average removes noise spikes from the recording, then a smoothing spline fits a 3D path as a function of time.
  5. Physics simulation. A PyBullet arm model follows the recorded path.

Low-voltage battery assessment, UofT Formula Racing

Python, Matplotlib, thermal modeling

I evaluated battery cell candidates for the low-voltage system of the team’s formula vehicle. I researched the options and ran simulations based on sample data from previous years.

  • Cells were compared on energy density and on running temperature, because competition rules strictly limit battery temperature.
  • Temperature was modeled with a thermal balance, where heat generated by I²R is offset by convective loss to the surroundings.
  • 17 feasible cells were simulated against the rules, including minimum cell voltage, maximum temperature and peak current. Simulated results for the cells were very close, so the recommendation was decided on energy density.

Cycloidal drive CAD model

3D CAD, speed reduction, robotics actuation

A cycloidal drive uses an eccentric bearing to make a cycloidal disc roll against pins around the outside of the housing. The output turns opposite to the input. It offers minimal backlash, high torque and a compact size, which suits robotic joints.

I used two discs instead of one, since a single disc is unbalanced and likely to cause vibration.
