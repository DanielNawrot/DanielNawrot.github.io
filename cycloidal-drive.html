Cycloidal drive CAD model

3D CAD, speed reduction, robotic actuation

A cycloidal drive is an unusual approach to speed reduction. An eccentric bearing is attached to a cycloidal disc, which rolls against pins lining the outside of the drive. The output turns in the opposite direction to the input.

The advantages are minimal backlash, high torque and a compact size, which makes it a good fit for robotic joints.

Design

The reduction ratio depends on the number of roller pins and the number of lobes on the disc.

r = (P − L) / P P = number of roller pins L = number of lobes on the disc

I used two discs, because a single disc is unbalanced and likely to cause vibration.
