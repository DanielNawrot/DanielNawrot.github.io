Low-voltage battery assessment

UofT Formula Racing · Python, Matplotlib, thermal modeling

I assessed battery cell candidates for the low-voltage system of the UofT Formula Racing vehicle. The work combined research into the options with simulations based on sample data from previous years.

Method

Cells were compared on energy density and on running temperature, since competition rules strictly limit battery temperature. Temperature was modeled with a thermal balance, where heat generated by I²R losses is offset by convective loss to the surroundings.

dT/dt = (Q_gen − Q_loss) / C_th = ( I(t)²·R_int − h·A_surface·(T − T_ambient) ) / (ρ·c_p)

Candidates

I compared volumetric and gravimetric energy density across the candidate cells.

Plot: volumetric vs gravimetric energy density

Simulation

17 feasible cells were simulated against the system’s power demand profile. For each cell I tracked current, voltage, temperature and heat generation, and checked the results against safety limits such as minimum cell voltage and maximum temperature. All 17 passed.

Plots: simulated voltage, current, temperature, heat generation, plus summary

Recommendation

The simulation results were extremely similar across cells, so the recommendation was based on energy density.
